//!GRADIENTS:
-->A CSS Gradient is a way to display a smooth transition between two or more colors 
-->It is applied using the background or background-image property.

//!Types of CSS Gradients:

//!1)linear-gradient:
-->A Linear Gradient creates a smooth color transition in a straight line (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal).
Syntax: background:linear-gradient(direction, color1, color2, ...);
Example:background:linear-gradient(to left,orange,yellow,green,blue);
        background:linear-gradient(to top left,orange,yellow,green,blue);
        background-image:linear-gradient(to right,violet 0% 5%,indigo 5% 10%,blue 10% 15%);
        background-image:repeating-linear-gradient(to top right,violet 0% 1%,indigo 1% 2%);
Note: Default direction → to bottom

//!2)radial-gradient:
-->A Radial Gradient creates a color transition starting from a center point and spreading outward in a circular or elliptical shape
Syntax: background: radial-gradient(shape, color1, color2, ...);
Example:background: radial-gradient(ellipse,red,green,blue,orange,yellow);
        background: radial-gradient(circle,red,green,blue,orange,yellow);
        background: radial-gradient(circle,violet 0% 1%,indigo 1% 2%);
        background: repeating-radial-gradient(circle,violet 0% 1%,indigo 1% 2%);
Note:
-->Shape can be circle or ellipse
-->Default shape → ellipse

//!3)conic-gradient:
-->A Conic Gradient creates a color transition around a center point (like a pie chart or color wheel).
Syntax: background: conic-gradient(color1, color2, ...);
Example: background: conic-gradient(brown,yellow,purple,pink);
         background: conic-gradient(brown 0% 2%,yellow 2% 4%,purple 4% 6%,pink 6% 8%);
         background: repeating-conic-gradient(brown 0% 2%,yellow 2% 4%,purple 4% 6%,pink 6% 8%);
