//!Overflow:
-->The overflow property in CSS is used to control what happens when content exceeds the size of its container (box).
->It is mainly used when the content is larger than the element’s defined width/height.

//!Values of Overflow:

1)overflow: visible;
-->This is the default value.
-->The content is not clipped and may overflow outside the container and can overlap with other elements.

2)overflow: hidden;
-->The overflowing content is clipped (hidden).
-->No scrollbars are provided.
-->Useful when you want to restrict content strictly inside the container.

3)overflow: scroll; 
-->Scrollbars are always shown, even if content does not overflow.
-->Allows users to scroll and view the hidden content.
-->Can sometimes look unnecessary if content fits. 

4)overflow: auto;
-->Scrollbars are added only when needed (i.e., when content overflows). 
-->More user-friendly compared to scroll.

//!Important Note:
-->You can control overflow separately for each axis:
   overflow-x → horizontal direction
   overflow-y → vertical direction
-->Overflow works properly only when height/width is defined.
